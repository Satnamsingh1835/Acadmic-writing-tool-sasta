# Research Synthesist

A researcher-controlled synthesis layer integrated with the existing academic-writing system. Extraction records what sources say; synthesis compares source records; validation checks traceability and overclaiming.

Evidence levels: A direct evidence; B strong interpretation; C cross-source synthesis; D analytical possibility; E speculation. Generated academic prose should normally use A–C; D–E must remain labelled.

Commons are analysed by resource, institution, property regime, access, use, governance, authority, boundaries, exclusion, commoning, power, caste, gender, class, state and market. Social boycott is separated into legal definition, sociological definition, empirical practice and institutional mechanism. Punjab cases track shamlat/panchayat/common land, leasing/auction practices, mobilisation, institutions, resistance and state response without assuming a causal sequence.

The deterministic extractor is conservative and heuristic. Researchers should review and enrich source records before relying on synthesis.
